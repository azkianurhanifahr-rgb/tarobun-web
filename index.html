FAQ

Got a Question?

We're ready to answer everything you want to know about Tarobun.

What is Tarobun?

Tarobun is an Indonesian bakery brand known for its distinctive soft purple buns, playful fillings, drinks, and sharing formats made for everyday moments.

What is the Family Pack?

The Family Pack contains six buns in one box, designed for sharing with family, friends, or colleagues. Mix-and-match rules must follow current store policy.

Are all flavours available at every store?

Product availability may vary by location, time, and stock. Please check with the store or customer service for the latest information.

Is Tarobun halal certified?

PT Kreasi Roti Global holds Halal Certificate No. ID32210051625300426. Product and outlet coverage follows the latest official certificate attachments. For the latest coverage, please contact customer service.

Can I order Tarobun online?

Online availability depends on your location. Visit the Order Online page or select a store to see the delivery platforms currently available.

How long can I keep Tarobun at room temperature?

Butter Variant (Non-Filled) Tarobun Butter variants without filling can last for up to 1 day at room temperature. Before enjoying, you can also reheat the bun to bring back its soft and delicious texture. Filled / Cream Variants For variants with filling or cream, we recommend consuming them within 2 hours at room temperature. If you are not consuming them immediately, please store them in the refrigerator and consume them within a maximum of 6 hours to maintain their quality and freshness. Our cream fillings are sensitive to room temperature. Leaving them at room temperature for too long may affect the product's quality, texture, and freshness. Tip: For the best taste and quality, we recommend enjoying your Tarobun as soon as possible after receiving it!

Where can I found Tarobun stores?

You’ll find Tarobun across Greater Jabodetabek areas and Bandung, making it easy to grab your favorite treats wherever your day takes you. We're located in convenient spots like MRT, LRT, and Commuter Line stations, as well as selected shopping malls. Keep an eye on our social media and Store Locator for the latest locations!
